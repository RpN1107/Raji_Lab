Gantyada Lavanya joined the “Computational Biophysics and Integrative Modelling Lab”. Her research focuses on using Molecular Dynamics simulations to map how viral intrinsically disordered regions (specifically the E1A protein of HAdV) infiltrate and hijack human host cells to aid next-gen therapeutic design. She is deeply interested in ongoing research developments in structural biology and computational virology to keep her work at the cutting edge. Prior to joining the lab, she obtained her Integrated M.Sc. in Biotechnology from GITAM University and worked as a Molecular Biologist at Transasia Bio-Medicals, where her team developed India’s first in-house diagnostic kit for Monkeypox.

Outside the lab, Lavanya enjoys training in Indian classical dance, acting in theatre, creating digital content, and exploring the fascinating history of scientific breakthroughs over iced lattes.
